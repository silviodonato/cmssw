Data scope

One event; every digi of the exported product is included. Points sit at the reconstructed cell centre of gravity in global centimetres. ADC, TOT and TOA are raw digi words of the selected sample; TOA is the raw 10-bit time-of-arrival count (0 usually means no TOA was recorded). ADC and TOT ranges apply only to digis in that mode. The whole dataset is embedded in this file; no server or internet connection is required.
